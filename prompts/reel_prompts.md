# Prompt library: talk to Claude + Blender MCP

Paste these into Claude Desktop or Claude Code (started inside this folder) while Blender
is open with the BlenderMCP add-on connected. Swap the `[brackets]`.

## Quick wins with the built-in templates
- *"Build reels/neon_orb.json in Blender, show me a viewport screenshot, then change the palette to sunset oranges and pinks."*
- *"Make a new kinetic_title reel with the hook 'ChatGPT can't do this' / 'but Claude can' / 'watch'. 5 seconds, cyan and lime accents."*
- *"Create a crystal_loop variant with a 12x12 grid, a slower wave and a gold/teal palette. Save it as reels/gold_wave.json."*

## Bespoke scenes (Claude writes bpy code live)
- *"Create a floating holographic AI brain made of glowing wireframe icospheres, slowly rotating, with data particles flowing into it. Vertical 9:16, seamless 8 s loop."*
- *"Build a 'prompt to reality' reel: a glowing text prompt types out at the top, then a low-poly city assembles block by block below it. 10 s."*
- *"Make a liquid chrome blob morphing with displacement noise on a black studio floor with soft reflections. Loop it perfectly over 6 s."*
- *"Recreate my concept: [describe your idea]. Keep the subject inside the Instagram safe zone and show me a screenshot before animating."*

## Using assets (enable them in the BlenderMCP panel first)
- *"Get a Poly Haven studio HDRI and a concrete floor texture, and put a slowly turning glossy robot head (generated with Hyper3D) in the centre."*

## Series ideas for an AI page
| Series | Template / idea | Hook example |
|---|---|---|
| AI tool of the day | kinetic_title over a neon_orb loop | "This AI replaces 3 apps" |
| Prompt -> Render | bespoke: text prompt, then the scene builds | "I typed one sentence..." |
| Satisfying AI loops | crystal_loop variants | (no text, just trending audio) |
| AI facts | kinetic_title | "AI did WHAT in 2026?" |

## Iterating
- *"Too busy. Remove half the sparks and make the background darker."*
- *"Hook text lands too late. Make it appear by frame 10."*
- *"Render a draft of frames 1-60 so I can check the timing."*
- *"Write me an Instagram caption and hashtags for this reel."*
