# Story draft: "AI wins debates when it knows who you are"

~29 s · 10 lines · status: **script ready**, shots not built yet

## Recording script (one take, ~1 s pause between lines)
1. 900 people sat down to debate hot-button issues.
2. Some debated a human. Some debated GPT-4.
3. But some of the AI's opponents came with something extra.
4. Basic personal info.
5. Who do you think won?
6. With that info, the AI won 64% of the debates.
7. Without it? No better than a human.
8. Your data isn't just for ads anymore. It's persuasion fuel.
9. 64% means debates won, not '64% more persuasive'.
10. Would you notice if an AI was changing your mind?
