# Story draft: "3 habits to use AI without losing your brain"

## Recording script (one take, ~1 s pause between lines)
1. Three studies. Three warning signs. Three habits.
2. Habit one: try it yourself first.
3. People who wrote with ChatGPT barely remembered their own words.
4. Habit two: ask for hints, not answers.
5. A hint-only AI tutor protected learning. Answer-copying hurt it.
6. Habit three: question the first answer.
7. Most people prompt once... and overrate the result.
8. Bonus: ask the AI to argue against you.
9. AI should make you sharper, not lazier.
10. Save this for the next time you open ChatGPT.
