# Story draft: "An AI talked people out of conspiracy theories"

~36 s · 11 lines · status: **script ready**, shots not built yet

## Recording script (one take, ~1 s pause between lines)
1. Researchers found 2,190 people who believed a conspiracy theory.
2. Then they let them argue with an AI.
3. No insults. No eye-rolling. Just patient facts, aimed at each person's argument.
4. Would anyone actually change their mind?
5. Belief dropped by about 20% on average.
6. About 1 in 4 stopped believing it altogether.
7. And two months later... it still held.
8. A fact-checker rated 99.2% of the AI's claims true.
9. The catch: the same skill could persuade people of false things too.
10. Facts can change minds, when they come with patience.
11. Would you let an AI try to change your mind?
