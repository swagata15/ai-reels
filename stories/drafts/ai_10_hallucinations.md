# Story draft: "Why AI confidently makes things up"

## Recording script (one take, ~1 s pause between lines)
1. Why does AI confidently make things up?
2. OpenAI researchers say it's partly how we test it.
3. Imagine a quiz: what's my birthday?
4. Guess, and you have a 1 in 365 chance of a point.
5. Say 'I don't know'... zero points. Every time.
6. Over thousands of questions, which strategy wins?
7. Guessing. So models learn to bluff.
8. The proposed fix: punish confident wrong answers more than honest uncertainty.
9. Newer models bluff less, but they still do.
10. So ask your AI: how sure are you?
11. What's the wildest thing an AI made up for you?
