# Story draft: "Can you quote your own essay?"

~33 s · 11 lines · status: **script ready**, shots not built yet

## Recording script (one take, ~1 s pause between lines)
1. Scientists wired 54 people's brains... then asked them to write.
2. One group used ChatGPT. One used Google. One used nothing.
3. Minutes later, they asked: can you quote your own essay?
4. Easy, right?
5. 83% of the ChatGPT group couldn't.
6. The others? Only about 1 in 10 struggled.
7. And their brain scans showed the lowest engagement of all.
8. When AI writes it, your brain doesn't bother to store it.
9. Small study, not peer-reviewed yet.
10. So write first... then let AI edit.
11. When did you last write something without AI?
