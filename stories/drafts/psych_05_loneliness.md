# Story draft: "The heaviest ChatGPT users were the loneliest"

~34 s · 11 lines · status: **script ready**, shots not built yet

## Recording script (one take, ~1 s pause between lines)
1. Millions of people now talk to ChatGPT about their feelings.
2. So OpenAI and MIT looked at about 40 million conversations...
3. then followed 981 people for four weeks.
4. Does talking to AI make you less lonely?
5. For most people, emotional chats were rare.
6. But the heaviest daily users reported the most loneliness...
7. more dependence, and less time with real people.
8. Careful: that's a correlation.
9. Lonely people may simply reach for AI more.
10. Use AI as a tool, not a replacement for people.
11. Have you ever talked to an AI when you felt alone?
