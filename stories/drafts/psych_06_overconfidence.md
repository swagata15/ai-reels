# Story draft: "AI made everyone overconfident"

~32 s · 12 lines · status: **script ready**, shots not built yet

## Recording script (one take, ~1 s pause between lines)
1. 500 people took a law-school logic test.
2. Half of them could use ChatGPT.
3. Then everyone guessed their own score.
4. You'd think AI users would know they had help, right?
5. AI did raise their scores...
6. but they thought they did even better.
7. The Dunning-Kruger effect vanished. Everyone overrated themselves.
8. Most asked the AI just once per question.
9. And the most AI-savvy were the most overconfident.
10. That last part is a correlation.
11. So ask twice. Check once more.
12. Do you double-check what AI tells you?
