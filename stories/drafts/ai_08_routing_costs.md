# Story draft: "How companies are cutting their AI bill"

~37 s · 11 lines · status: **script ready**, shots not built yet

## Recording script (one take, ~1 s pause between lines)
1. Every time you ask an AI something, someone pays for it.
2. And companies are quietly cutting that bill.
3. The trick? Not every question needs the biggest model.
4. What's two plus two? A tiny, cheap model can do that.
5. A tricky legal question? Send it to the big one.
6. That's called routing.
7. In a Berkeley study, a router kept 95% of GPT-4's quality...
8. while sending only about a quarter of questions to GPT-4.
9. Add smaller models and caching, and costs fall further.
10. Savings vary by task, but the direction is clear.
11. Which do you really need: the giant, or the specialist?
