# Story draft: "AI made students worse at math (one kind of AI)"

~35 s · 12 lines · status: **script ready**, shots not built yet

## Recording script (one take, ~1 s pause between lines)
1. Nearly 1,000 students got ChatGPT to help them study math.
2. Their practice scores shot up.
3. Then the AI was taken away... for the exam.
4. They should do better, right?
5. They scored 17% worse than students who never had AI.
6. They'd used it like a crutch, copying answers.
7. But here's the twist.
8. Another group got an AI tutor that only gave hints.
9. And their exam scores held up.
10. AI didn't hurt learning. AI doing the thinking did.
11. One school, one subject. But a clear lesson.
12. Do you ask AI for answers... or for hints?
