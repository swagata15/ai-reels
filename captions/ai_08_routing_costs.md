How companies are cutting AI costs without losing quality 💸

AI at the same quality level has been getting ~10x cheaper every year (a16z), and companies are saving even more with ROUTING:

→ Easy question → small, cheap model. Hard question → big model
→ UC Berkeley's RouteLLM kept ~95% of GPT-4's quality while sending only ~26% of questions to GPT-4 (up to 85% cheaper on one benchmark)
→ Next levers: small language models (SLMs) + caching repeated prompts

⚠️ Caveat: savings vary a lot by task and benchmark.
