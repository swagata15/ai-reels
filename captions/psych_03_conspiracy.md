A chatbot talked people out of conspiracy theories 🤯

2,190 people who believed a conspiracy theory debated it with GPT-4.

→ Belief dropped by about 20% on average
→ About 1 in 4 no longer believed it at all
→ The effect lasted 2+ months
→ A professional fact-checker rated 99.2% of the AI's claims true

What worked: patient, evidence-based answers tailored to each person's exact argument.

⚠️ Caveat: beliefs were reduced, not erased, and the authors warn the same persuasive power could be misused.
