AI made students 17% worse at math, but only one kind of AI 📉

About 1,000 high-school students practiced math with GPT-4 and then took the exam without it.

→ Students who had unrestricted ChatGPT-style access scored 17% lower than students who never had AI
→ They used it as a crutch, copying answers instead of learning
→ A tutor version that gave HINTS instead of answers removed the harm

⚠️ Caveat: one school in Turkey, one subject (math).
