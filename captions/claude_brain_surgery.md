This tiny mouse just had brain surgery... and no human chose the treatment 🧠🤖

San Francisco startup Exin Therapeutics says it gave an AI agent (built on Claude Sonnet 5) a list of 19 gene therapies, an epileptic mouse, and control of a robotic needle. One goal: reduce the seizures.

→ The first Claude model they tried (Opus 5.5) planned the surgery, then reportedly refused to guide the needle
→ The agent picked a therapy that boosts GABA, the brain's natural "brakes", and targeted the lateral entorhinal cortex
→ A vet opened the skull; the AI steered the needle in and injected 250 nanoliters, a droplet smaller than a pinhead
→ The company says two other AIs given the same task each picked a different treatment

⚠️ These are company claims with no peer-reviewed results yet. The mouse recovered, but whether the seizures dropped is still unknown.
