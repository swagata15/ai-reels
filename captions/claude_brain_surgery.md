An AI agent just picked a gene therapy and injected it into a living brain 🧠🤖

Exin Therapeutics, a San Francisco biotech startup, gave an agent built on Claude Sonnet 5 a
list of 19 possible gene therapies, an epileptic mouse, and control of a robotic injector.
Its only goal: reduce the seizures.

It chose a GABA-boosting therapy, targeted the lateral entorhinal cortex, and injected 250 nL.

The fine print 👇
• A human vet surgeon opened the skull first
• This is company-reported, with no peer-reviewed paper yet
• Whether the seizures actually dropped is still unknown
