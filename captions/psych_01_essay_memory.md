83% couldn't quote their own essay 🧠

MIT Media Lab tracked the brain activity (EEG) of 54 people while they wrote essays, using ChatGPT, Google, or nothing at all.

→ 83% of the ChatGPT group couldn't quote a sentence they had written minutes earlier, vs about 11% in the other groups
→ ChatGPT users also showed the lowest brain engagement

When AI writes it, your brain doesn't store it.

⚠️ Caveat: small study, a preprint that isn't peer-reviewed yet, and the authors warn against "AI makes you dumb" headlines.
