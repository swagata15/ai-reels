Why AI confidently makes things up 🤥

OpenAI researchers explain it with a test analogy:

→ AI models are scored like a multiple-choice exam
→ Guess someone's birthday and you have a 1-in-365 chance of a point
→ Say "I don't know" and you get 0 points, every time
→ So models learn to bluff

Proposed fix: penalize confident errors more than honest uncertainty.

⚠️ This is OpenAI's own explanation; newer models hallucinate less, but still do.
