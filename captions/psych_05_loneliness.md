The heaviest ChatGPT users were the loneliest 💙

OpenAI and MIT Media Lab studied emotional use of ChatGPT: about 40 million conversations analyzed, plus a 4-week study with 981 people.

→ Emotional conversations were rare overall
→ But the heaviest daily users reported more loneliness, more emotional dependence and less time with real people

⚠️ Caveat: this is a correlation. Lonely people may simply use AI more. Both studies are preprints.
