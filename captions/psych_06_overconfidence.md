AI made everyone overconfident 🫠

About 500 people solved law-school (LSAT) logic problems, half of them with ChatGPT.

→ AI raised their scores, but they overestimated how well they did
→ The classic Dunning-Kruger pattern disappeared: everyone overrated themselves
→ Most users prompted only once per question
→ The more AI-savvy people were, the more overconfident they tended to be

⚠️ Caveat: the AI-literacy finding is a correlation, not proof of cause.
